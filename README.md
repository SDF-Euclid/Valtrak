OVERVIEW:

Valtrak is a turn based military card game where 2 players go head-to-head in combat to see who is victorious. The game is still in its rough draft stage, but I have a lot of ideas about how I want it to play. I 
want the feel to be similar to Magic or Pokémon, but for Valtrak to offer a unique enough experience/feel to stand as its own game for people who enjoy military combat. Also as of right now I am only focusing on
adding real vehicles, but I think in the future it would be fun to have WWI or WWII vehicles or even fantasy vehicles and have different subsections of Valtrak so if you wanted to play exclusively WWII era, you could.

GAME FLOW:

The feel I have for the flow would be each player has their deck (I was thinking between 70-80 cards, but can be subject to change. I'll also probably add some more rules about card appearance and other stuff). 
The starting hand size and rules are also not completely final yet, but I was thinking 7ish cards to start, and you need at least one ground deployable vehicle. Each player starts their turn by drawing a card. 
From there if they have any ground deployable units, they can place them face down on their side of the playfield and that forms a "Strike Group". A Strike Group can consist of 6 vehicles total, with limitations 
being only 1 specialist vehicle and 1 resupply vehicle (unless commander bonus says otherwise). The reason the vehicle card is face down is so that the opponent doesn't know what the vehicle is, but they know
something is there. The person who played the vehicle card can look at it at any time. In order for a Strike Group to attack another Strike Group, the attacking group needs to be "revealed" (flipped over) and the
Strike Group needs to have enough ammunition to attack (think required energy in Pokémon or mana in Magic). If a Strike Group is destroyed, the attacking player takes a territory chip. For each territory chip a
player has, they are able to deploy one extra Strike Group. There are 7 total territory chips, and the first player to take 5 territory chips wins (still under consideration).
